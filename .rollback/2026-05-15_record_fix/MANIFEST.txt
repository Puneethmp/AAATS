WHY
---
Two unrelated bugs bundled into one atomic deploy because both touch
image-baked code (trading/, execution/) and a single rebuild covers both:

1. P1 — SELL-shares mismatch (root cause of TON/FET reconcile residuals)
   Strategies C3 and C6 recorded SELL rows with shares = size_usd / SELL_price
   instead of the BUY row's entry shares. SUM(BUY) - SUM(SELL) leaves dust
   per closed position. C3 had been firing; C6 was latent (no losing-TRX
   round-trip yet).

2. P2 — Loguru printf-format sweep
   `execution/paper_trader.py` had 3 `_log.<level>(...%s %d %.4f, args)` calls
   (loguru wants `{}` format). All INFO/WARN, all on the trade-write hot path.
   Operator was seeing literal `%s %s @ %.4f x%.6f` in trade logs since the
   last paper_trader edit.
