WHY
---
The C3+C6 `_record` exit-sizing fix (deploy 2026-05-15_record_fix, image
f19fa108e97a...) eliminates the SHARES = size_usd / exit_price bug-class but
relies on every strategy author remembering to pass entry shares. A permanent
post-write detector closes that loop: any future SELL whose shares != the
FIFO-matched BUY shares fires a single WARN line, regardless of which
strategy wrote it. Bug-class-level guardrail, not a single-strategy patch.

Detection-only by design — HALT/reconcile decisions still belong to the
reconciler and the upcoming unified-positions-ledger migration.
